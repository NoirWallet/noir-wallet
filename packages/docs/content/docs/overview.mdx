Noir Wallet is a self-custodial Zcash wallet for Chrome. It keeps wallet credentials encrypted on your device while making shielded ZEC practical for everyday wallet use and dApp integrations.

## What you can do

- Create or restore multiple wallets and accounts.
- Send and receive ZEC with transparent or shielded addresses.
- Shield transparent funds inside the wallet.
- On mainnet, swap supported assets and access lending flows.
- Connect supported dApps through the injected Noir provider.
- Use a Keystone or Ledger hardware wallet for supported transaction flows.

## Privacy model

Zcash privacy depends on the address and funding source used for a transaction. Shielded addresses and shielded funds provide the strongest privacy. Transparent transactions are visible on-chain and can reveal relationships between addresses.

<Callout type="warn" title="Self-custody means responsibility">
  Noir Wallet cannot recover your seed phrase. Store it offline and never share it with a website,
  support account, or person.
</Callout>
