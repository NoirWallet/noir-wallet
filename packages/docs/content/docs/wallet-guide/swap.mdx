title: Swap
description: Review swap quotes, fund the quoted deposit, and follow cross-chain settlement.

---

On mainnet, Noir Wallet can route supported swaps through external liquidity and cross-chain services. Availability, price, and settlement time depend on the selected assets and external services.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose assets and amount

    Select the asset you are paying and the asset you want to receive.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review the quote

    Check the selected destination or refund address before requesting a quote. On **Review Quote**,
    check the estimated output, minimum received, price impact, slippage, and any ZEC network fee.
    A quote can change before submission.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fund the quoted deposit

    Approve the wallet's transfer from the selected source account to the quoted deposit address.
    Depending on the chain and account, this may require a hardware-signing step. Check the source
    network and token before approval; sending another token or using another network can prevent
    a refund. The wallet then monitors the deposit or relay.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Follow activity

    Check **Swap progress** or Activity until the destination result reaches its final state. A
    swap into ZEC can still wait for Zcash confirmations after the relay succeeds.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Cross-chain swaps depend on public RPC, liquidity, and relay services outside Noir Wallet. A
  delayed status does not always mean the origin transaction failed.
</Callout>
