Shielding creates a transaction from your transparent balance into the Zcash shielded pool. Future transactions funded from the shielded balance can provide stronger privacy.

## Shield transparent ZEC

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open **Shield Funds**

    The action appears on Home when the selected account has enough confirmed transparent ZEC. It
    also appears under **Addresses** as **Transfer to Shielded Address**.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review the transaction

    The confirmation sheet shows the confirmed transparent balance to shield and lets you select
    a network fee tier. This action shields the available transparent balance; there is no amount
    field.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Approve and wait for confirmation

    The transparent source remains visible on-chain. Wait for the shield transaction to confirm
    before spending the resulting shielded funds.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What shielding does not do

- It cannot remove a transparent transaction that is already on-chain.
- It does not protect information shared outside the wallet.
- It does not make unrelated browser activity anonymous.

<Callout type="info">
  For the strongest Zcash privacy, receive to a shielded address and spend from shielded funds
  whenever the other service supports it.
</Callout>
